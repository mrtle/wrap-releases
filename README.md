# Wrap for Mac

Wrap swaps your personal details for realistic fakes before you paste into an AI, and puts them back in the answer. Everything happens on your Mac.

- **Download:** [latest release](https://github.com/mrtle/wrap-releases/releases/latest) (macOS 14 or later, signed and notarized by Apple)
- **Mac App Store:** coming soon
- **Support:** hello@neocast.us · [support page](https://mrtle.github.io/wrap-releases/support.html)
- **Privacy:** [privacy policy](https://mrtle.github.io/wrap-releases/privacy.html)
