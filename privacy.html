Privacy Policy

Wrap for Mac // Neocast Inc. // Effective September 29, 2026

The short version: Wrap works on your Mac. We don't collect, store, sell or share your personal information, and nothing you copy or drop into Wrap is ever sent to us.

What stays on your Mac

The text you copy, the files you wrap or restore, the fake details Wrap makes (stand-ins), the fake details you add yourself, and your history. Wrap reads and changes these only on your Mac. The part of Wrap that reads your text has no networking code at all. Your stand-ins and history are encrypted on your Mac with a key kept in your Keychain, and deleted on the schedule you choose in Settings.

What goes online, and why

  • License checks (direct download only). When you activate a Wrap+ or Founders license key, and about once a day after that, Wrap sends the key and an anonymous ID for your Mac ("Mac" plus 8 characters made by hashing the Mac's hardware ID) to our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy, to confirm the license. Your Mac's name, serial number and anything you copied are never sent.
  • Update checks (direct download only). About once a day Wrap downloads a small file that says whether a new version exists. The request carries no identifiers. The file is hosted on GitHub, which may log your IP address as described in GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Purchases. In the Mac App Store version, Apple handles purchases and subscriptions; we don't receive your payment details. In the direct download version, Lemon Squeezy is the merchant of record and handles payment, receipts and taxes under its own privacy policy; we receive the order details it shares with sellers (such as the email you bought with) to provide your license and support.

The Mac App Store version makes no network requests of its own.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • No account is needed to use Wrap.
  • We don't sell or share personal information.

Your choices

You can delete all of Wrap's history at any time in Settings › History › Wipe everything, or remove Wrap from your Mac. To ask about the order details Lemon Squeezy shares with us, or to have them deleted, contact us below.

Children

Wrap is a general-audience productivity app and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
