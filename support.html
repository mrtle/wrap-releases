How Wrap works

  1. Copy your message, like always.
  2. Paste into AI. In Wrapped mode, Wrap has already swapped your details for fake ones.
  3. Put your info back. Paste the AI's answer or drop the file it made into Wrap. Your real details go back in.

Common questions

The dot is red. Your real details would go out if you pasted now. Turn on Wrapped in the menu bar window. On the Free plan, names and addresses need Wrap+.
My license key says it's in use on another Mac. Each license works on one Mac. On the old Mac, open Wrap › Settings › Plan › Deactivate this Mac, then activate the key on the new one.
Wrap didn't put a name back. If the AI changed a stand-in (for example shortened it), Wrap leaves it alone and tells you, so nothing is replaced by mistake. If you typed your own fake details, add them under "Used your own fake details?" in the Wrap window.
Cancel or manage a subscription. Mac App Store: System Settings › your name › Media & Purchases › Subscriptions. Direct download: the link in your purchase email, or Wrap › Settings › Plan › Manage subscription.
